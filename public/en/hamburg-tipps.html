Hamburg by S1: tips from Wedel

The line

In Wedel you board at the first stop. The S1 runs to Landungsbrücken, the city centre and the main station without changing.

You need no car and no change of trains. The tips follow the order in which you reach the stops. Open a tip to see details and ticket links.

As of 14 November 2026. The routes are suggestions. Before your visit, check timetables, engineering works and opening hours with HVV and with the venue.

  1. Wedel

    Board at the first stop

    From stayHere 02 it is about a 5-minute walk to the S-Bahn station; from stayHere 03 it is about 12 minutes by bus. The train starts here.

  2. Blankenese

    Treppenviertel (stairs quarter) and Elbe beach

    Narrow stairways lead down between old captains' houses to the Elbe beach. This is also where you arrive on the Elbe riverside path from Wedel.

  3. Landungsbrücken

    Harbour and Elbphilharmonie

    The harbour ferries leave from here. For the Port Anniversary in May you also get off here.

    Elbphilharmonie PlazaHafenCity, Platz der Deutschen Einheit

    The viewing platform looks out over the city and harbour. From Landungsbrücken take the U3 to Baumwall and walk from there. The Plaza ticket is not a concert ticket and does not include public transport.

    Plaza ticketsDirections

  4. Stadthausbrücke

    Speicherstadt

    From here you walk into the Speicherstadt warehouse district.

    Miniatur WunderlandSpeicherstadt, Kehrwieder 2

    Model railways and miniature worlds on several floors. It is best to reserve your time slot in advance. From Stadthausbrücke it is about an 11-minute walk according to the venue.

    TicketsDirections

    Hamburg DungeonSpeicherstadt, Kehrwieder 2

    Live actors and scary stories from Hamburg's history. Book a time slot online and check the notes on age and language.

    TicketsGetting here

  5. Jungfernstieg

    Alster lake and HafenCity

    The Inner Alster lake is at Jungfernstieg. From here the U4 runs to HafenCity.

    Westfield Hamburg-ÜberseequartierHafenCity, near the Speicherstadt

    Shopping, food and leisure under one roof. From Jungfernstieg take the U4 towards Elbbrücken to Überseequartier. You need no reservation for shopping.

    Information and offersDirections

  6. Hauptbahnhof (main station)

    Long-distance trains and on to the airport

    Here you change to long-distance trains. The S1 continues to Hamburg Airport. More under Getting here.
