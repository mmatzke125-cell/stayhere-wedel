By S1

Wedel is the last stop of the S1. From there you travel to Hamburg city centre and the airport without changing.

Check journey times, frequency and engineering works in the HVV timetable. What lies at the stops is described in the Hamburg tips.

By train
Long-distance trains stop at Hamburg main station. From there the S1 runs directly to Wedel.
By plane
From Hamburg Airport the S1 runs to Wedel without changing. In the other direction the train is split at Ohlsdorf, so make sure you are in the section for the airport.
By car
All three rentals have a free parking space. You receive the address for your sat nav after booking.
Late arrival
The host will pick you up at Wedel S-Bahn station by arrangement.

Frequent questions

Where are the stayHere places located?

All three are in 22880 Wedel in Schleswig-Holstein, right on Hamburg's western city limits and on the Elbe. stayHere 02 is in the centre, stayHere 03 in a quiet residential area and stayHere 01 on the edge of the Klövensteen forest. You receive the exact address after booking.

The three rentals

Which place suits how many guests?

stayHere 01 is for up to 4 guests (2 bedrooms), stayHere 02 for up to 3 guests (1 bedroom and a single bed in the living room) and stayHere 03 for up to 6 guests (4 bedrooms).

Comparison of the rentals

How far is it to the S-Bahn?

From stayHere 02 it is about a 5-minute walk to Wedel S-Bahn station. From stayHere 03 it is about 12 minutes by bus. For stayHere 01 you plan the route from the address with HVV. If you arrive late, the host will pick you up at the station by arrangement.

Comparison of the rentals

How do I get from Wedel to Hamburg?

By S1 from Wedel S-Bahn station. It runs without changing via Blankenese and Altona to Reeperbahn, Landungsbrücken, Jungfernstieg and the main station. Wedel is the last stop of the line. HVV shows current departures and engineering works.

Hamburg by S1

How do I get from Hamburg Airport to Wedel?

The S1 links Hamburg Airport and Wedel without changing. On the way to the airport the train is split at Ohlsdorf: one section goes to the airport, the other to Poppenbüttel. So check the display on the train.

Is there parking?

Yes, free at all three. stayHere 01 has parking on the property, stayHere 02 a parking space at the building and stayHere 03 a reserved space right at the house.

Which place has a balcony or garden?

stayHere 02 has a sunny south-facing balcony and a shared garden with a barbecue area. stayHere 03 has its own garden. stayHere 01 is on the edge of the forest.

The three rentals

Is one of the places low-barrier?

stayHere 02 is on the 3rd floor, reached by lift and largely step-free. There is one small step at the shower and one at the balcony. So it has few barriers but is not fully accessible.

stayHere 02: access without stairs

Can I use the pool table?

The pool table belongs to stayHere 01. It is not available in winter. For the rest of the year the host confirms its use before you arrive.

stayHere 01: pool table and movie night

How far are the Elbe and the dyke?

From stayHere 03 it is about 12 minutes by bus to the Schulauer Fährhaus on the Elbe. stayHere 02 is in the centre near the Elbe. The Elbe dyke leads west from Wedel into the Wedel and Haseldorf marshes.

Elbe dyke and Wedel marshes

Is stayHere 02 also rented for single nights?

No. stayHere 02 is now rented only from one month, furnished, through Wunderflats for 1 to 6 months. The current rent is shown in the listing. For shorter stays there are stayHere 01 and stayHere 03.

stayHere 02: rent and terms

How do I book and where do I see prices?

You book stayHere 01 and stayHere 03 through Airbnb. You rent stayHere 02 from one month through Wunderflats. Each platform shows prices, available dates and terms.

In German, English and Spanish. On Airbnb he usually replies within an hour.
