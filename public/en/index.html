The idea

Stay somewhere quiet on the edge of Hamburg, between the Elbe and the forest, and still reach the city on a single train.

stayHere is three privately run places to stay in Wedel: a basement apartment for 4 guests by the forest, a terraced house for 6 guests with a garden and an apartment for 3 guests near the S-Bahn. Each has its own kitchen and a free parking space. You can book the basement apartment and the terraced house for single nights too. The apartment near the S-Bahn is rented from one month.

This site tells your stay in three chapters. You arrive at your rental, look around Wedel and the Elbe, and take the S1 into Hamburg.
