The calendar

Trade fairs, concerts, sport and the Port Anniversary, all reachable by S-Bahn from Wedel.

For each date you find the link to the organiser and the rentals that suit it. Which stops the S1 serves is described in the Hamburg tips.

Notes on dates, tickets and available nights

The dates come from the organisers' websites, as of 14 November 2026. Changes are possible.

stayHere does not sell tickets. A recommendation does not mean the rental is free on that date. Available nights are shown on Airbnb and Wunderflats.
