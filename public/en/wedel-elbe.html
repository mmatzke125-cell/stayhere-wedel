The place

On the doorstep: a river with big ships, a dyke with wide views and a forest.

Wedel is in Schleswig-Holstein, just beyond Hamburg's city limits. Feel free to plan a whole day without Hamburg.

The tips are sorted by place: first to the water, then onto the dyke, along the bank, into the forest and finally into town.

By the waterOn the dykeAlong the bankIn the forestIn town

By the water

Willkomm-Höft at the Schulauer Fährhaus

At the ship greeting station, vessels heading to or from Hamburg on the Elbe are welcomed and seen off. From the bank you see container ships and cruise ships up close.

From stayHere 03 it is about 12 minutes by bus.

Visitor information from the town of Wedel

By the water

Strandbar 28° and Hamburg Yacht Harbour

The beach bar is at Wedel's river beach right on the Elbe. Opening hours depend on the weather; the operator confirms reservations.

Despite its name, the Hamburg Yacht Harbour is in Wedel. A stroll through the harbour area is worthwhile for the many sailing and motor boats. Please observe the access rules on site.

On the dyke

Elbe dyke and Wedel marshes

West of Wedel the dyke landscape of the Elbe marshes begins. On the dyke you walk or cycle between the Elbe and the marsh meadows towards Fährmannssand and the Haseldorf marshes.

In the Wedel marshes the conservation group NABU runs a bird station with observation hides. In winter thousands of geese rest there. The station is open only on certain days of the week.

NABU bird station Wedel marshes

Along the bank

Elbe riverside path towards Blankenese

The path starts at the Schulauer Fährhaus and follows the water eastwards. You decide along the way how far to go. You return on foot or by S1 from Blankenese.

What awaits you in Blankenese is described in Chapter 3.

Elbe riverside path, town of Wedel

In the forest

Klövensteen forest and wildlife park

The Klövensteen forest borders Wedel. stayHere 01 is right on the edge of the forest. The wildlife park belongs to Hamburg-Rissen. Check opening hours and possible closures before you go.

Klövensteen wildlife park

In town

Roland, Barlach and Bahnhofstraße

On the market square stands the Roland statue, the town's landmark. In the birthplace of the sculptor Ernst Barlach at Mühlenstraße 1, the Barlach art museum shows changing exhibitions.

On Bahnhofstraße you find cafés, pharmacies and shops. stayHere 02 is in the centre, about a 5-minute walk from the S-Bahn station.
